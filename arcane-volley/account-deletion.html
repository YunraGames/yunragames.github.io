Delete Your Arcane Volley Account and Data

Arcane Volley is developed and published by Yunra Games. This page explains how to request deletion of your Arcane Volley account and associated game data.

Request Deletion

  1. Email yunragames@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Unity Player Account.
  2. Use the subject Arcane Volley Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you want your Arcane Volley account and associated cloud data deleted. Do not send a password, payment-card information, or other sensitive information.
  4. We may ask for limited information needed to verify account ownership and prevent unauthorized deletion.

Request Account Deletion

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your Arcane Volley Unity Authentication player record.
  • Cloud-saved game progress associated with that player, including progression, currencies, unlocked content, achievements, settings, and other stored gameplay data.
  • Other Arcane Volley account data controlled by Yunra Games that is associated with the verified account.

Data That May Be Retained

  • Purchase and transaction records controlled by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or other service providers are governed by those providers' policies.
  • Records required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial reporting may be retained only for the period required for those purposes.
  • Deletion-request correspondence may be retained as needed to document and complete the request.

Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days. We will tell you if applicable law permits or requires additional time.

Local Device Data

Cloud deletion does not automatically erase files already stored on a device. To remove local Arcane Volley data, clear the app's storage in device settings or uninstall the app after the cloud deletion is complete.

Unity Player Account

Deleting Arcane Volley game data and deleting the separate Unity Player Account are different actions. To also delete your Unity Player Account, use Unity's account deletion page.

Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, email Yunra Games at yunragames@gmail.com.
